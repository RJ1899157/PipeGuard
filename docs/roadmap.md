# Delivery roadmap

This roadmap records product delivery milestones. Stage numbers correspond to the original thirteen-week plan; they are not fixed calendar deadlines or a claim that a stage is complete.

**Current state:** persistence and version 1 internal execution ingestion verified on PostgreSQL. Local HTTP API, scoped producer authentication, restricted runtime role and provisioning are verified. The independent orders pipeline is verified. Airflow scheduling and the bounded workload event adapter are verified. Quality-result contracts, deterministic incident detection, independent Airflow reconciliation and coverage health are verified. Bounded OpenLineage ingestion, historical traversal, lineage-delivery expectations and incident evidence snapshots are implemented and verified. The local read-only dashboard foundation is implemented with an initial attempt-scoped React Flow view. Stage 9 exploration is verified: execution timeline, immutable quality-report charts and representative rendered graph checks. The interruption recovery and cross-system audit are recorded in the system audit report. The pre-investigation hardening checkpoint adds independent local monitoring, verified restore tooling, bounded captured pagination and nonce CSP; see hardening verification. Stage 10 now has durable manual Groq investigations, scoped API/history, dashboard controls and bounded grounding checks. The bounded local advisory gate now passes 36 offline evaluation checks and six fixed repeated live trials with GPT-OSS 120B; broader live incident-class coverage remains limited. Stage 11 now has durable review-only proposals and human decisions through scoped APIs and the dashboard; the dedicated Airflow recovery account and disabled scoped permissions are verified; the separate executable CLI and supervised dashboard/API flow, durable intent and exact-run reconciliation are implemented and tested in isolation; execution remains disabled pending controlled live acceptance.

## Review gates

- Review database ownership and the logical model before committing to the first schema.
- Validate the pipeline-to-application event contract before broad adapter work.
- Establish evidence provenance before introducing AI hypotheses.
- Review the dashboard design direction before expanding all product screens.
- Evaluate investigation quality and approval controls before connecting recovery execution.
- Complete an integrated demonstration and final security/performance review before claiming production readiness.

## Scope controls

Keep initial execution local with synthetic data. Cloud integrations, multi-tenant deployment, extensive distributed processing, and 3D exploration are optional extensions that require their own scope and acceptance criteria. Optimize from measured workloads rather than assuming one design is universally optimal.
